## Why digital products belong here

Found & Forged is designed as more than a service site. The long-term headquarters includes calculators, learning hubs, templates, and plans that help homeowners make better decisions before money is spent.

## The first products

Early digital products will focus on gravel driveway planning, small deck preparation, material estimating, and practical property maintenance checklists.

## Built to stay useful

The goal is simple: fewer vague instructions, more field-ready guidance, and a brand library that feels as dependable as the work itself.
