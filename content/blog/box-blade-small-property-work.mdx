## Box blades are surface tools

A box blade can cut high spots, pull loose material, reshape small areas, and leave a rough grade ready for finish work. It is especially useful for gravel drives, utility lanes, and compact property projects.

## Drainage still matters

Moving material without thinking about water can make a clean-looking surface fail quickly. Before grading, identify where runoff should go and where it currently collects.

## Work in passes

Good grading is rarely one dramatic pass. Smaller passes create more control, protect the base, and make the finished surface more predictable.
